# MyExperiment.club Brand And Landing Page Brief

## 1. One-line product summary
MyExperiment.club is a managed subscription website platform that gives local service businesses a professional site, booking/request tools, and ongoing support in one simple monthly package.

## 2. Short elevator pitch
MyExperiment.club helps local service businesses go live with a professional website without the usual complexity, agency cost, or maintenance burden. Customers choose their industry, preview a realistic demo site, customise key details, and submit setup in a guided flow.

The platform is designed around practical business outcomes: more enquiries, more bookings, better customer communication, and easier day-to-day operations. It is not just a brochure website. It includes service/pricing presentation, booking or quote flows, staff and schedule tooling, and customer interaction support.

The commercial model is intentionally simple: one full package for everyone, with no confusing feature tiers. The platform handles hosting, setup support, domain guidance, and ongoing management so business owners can stay focused on running their business.

For design work, the product should feel trustworthy, modern, practical, and approachable to small local businesses across multiple industries.

## 3. Target customers
Primary audience:
- Small and local service businesses.
- Owners/operators who need a clear online presence plus practical tools for bookings, quotes, staff scheduling, and customer contact.
- Businesses that want a managed solution instead of piecing together multiple tools.

## 4. Core customer journey
1. Business owner chooses their industry on MyExperiment.club.
2. They view an industry-specific demo site.
3. They customise core details (business identity, services, key content).
4. They submit a setup request.
5. They choose domain path:
   - use existing domain (customer points DNS/nameservers), or
   - request domain registration/management.
6. Setup/subscription payment process is completed at onboarding stage.
7. Platform team provisions the subscriber site and tracks setup/domain progress.
8. Business owner manages services, staff, availability, and operational settings.
9. End customers use the live site to book/request services.

## 6. Pricing model
- Website setup: **£149** (one-time)
- Subscription: **£30/month**
- Domain registration/management: **£49** (only if MyExperiment.club registers/manages the domain)
- If customer already owns domain and can point DNS/nameservers: **no domain fee**
- Optional WhatsApp communication add-on: **+£10/month**
- Email notifications: **included as standard**
- Packaging model: **one simple full-feature package (no tiered plans)**

## 7. Logo/brand direction notes
Brand direction should communicate:
- Simple, trustworthy, modern, practical
- Suitable for local businesses (not overly enterprise/corporate)
- Multi-industry friendly (not visually locked to one trade)
- More than a website template: managed support + business operations value

Visual guidance:
- Prefer neutral base with one confident accent colour
- Clear, high-legibility typography
- Strong contrast across light and dark surfaces
- Friendly but professional tone

Logo system should work for:
- Full wordmark
- Small icon/favicon
- Social profile/avatar
- Header logo
- Invoice/email footer mark

## 10. Differentiators
- Not just a static template
- Not confusing tiered packages
- Demo/customise before subscribing
- Industry-specific workflows
- Business admin tooling included
- Managed setup, hosting, and domain support

## 11. What not to imply
- Do not imply live payment processing is fully implemented today unless presented as future capability.
- Do not imply every backend capability is production-complete where current modules are still mock/local.
- Do not overpromise domain automation or live Twilio/payment integrations.
- Keep messaging grounded in current customer-facing value and clear roadmap direction.
